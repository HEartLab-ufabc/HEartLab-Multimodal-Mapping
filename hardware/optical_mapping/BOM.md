# Optical Mapping BOM

Quantities are configuration dependent.

| Component | Quantity | Specification | Notes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| High-speed optical camera | 2–3 | High-speed optical-mapping camera | Camera count depends on configuration |
| Lens | 1 per camera | Approximately 25 mm | Used in the  optical-mapping implementation |
| Long-pass emission filter | 1 per camera | Approximately 715 nm | Positioned in the fluorescence collection path |
| Excitation LED | Up to 6 in the small-heart implementation | Approximately 650 nm | Quantity and placement depend on optical access |
| LED driver / power supply | As required | Compatible with excitation LEDs | Laboratory illumination supply |
| Camera mount | 1 per camera | Rigid adjustable support | Commercial/custom |
| LED mount | As required | Adjustable | Commercial/custom |
| Optical mirror | 1 for epi-endo | Mounted at approximately 45° | Provides additional optical access |
| Mirror holder | 1 | Custom | CAD under `cad/epi-endo/` |
| Trigger cable/interface | 1 per synchronized camera | Camera timing input | Electrical interface is camera specific |
