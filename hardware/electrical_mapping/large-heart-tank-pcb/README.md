# Large-Heart Tank Electrode PCB

This directory contains the KiCad design and manufacturing files for the custom electrode PCB used on the large-heart torso-tank mapping system.

The PCB forms one face of the large-heart tank electrode assembly.

## Tank arrangement

The large-heart whole-organ tank uses:

- six PCB faces;
- 15 electrode pads per PCB;
- 90 bath-potential electrodes when all six faces are populated.

The custom mechanical support for these boards is documented at:

[`../../../cad/large-heart/transparent-pcb-support/`](../../../cad/large-heart/transparent-pcb-support/)

## Current PCB design

The uploaded design contains:

- 15 circular electrode pads;
- one 1×16, 2.54 mm pitch horizontal pin-header footprint;
- one-to-one connections from electrode pads `U1`–`U15` to connector pins `1`–`15`;
- connector pin `16` with no corresponding electrode pad in the current PCB project;
- a custom `Round_Pad` footprint with a nominal 10 mm circular copper pad.

See:

[`electrode_mapping.csv`](electrode_mapping.csv)

## Board outline

The current KiCad edge-cuts geometry spans approximately:

- 303 mm in X;
- 115 mm in Y;

including the connector-tab region.

## Important: PCB material

> [!WARNING]
> The current `TankPCB_1.kicad_pcb` file declares a conventional 1.6 mm FR4 stack-up. This happens because KiCAD does not offer PET as manufacturing material. When ordering your board from your preferred manufacturer check their constraints and preferred file format.

## Electrode finish

The KiCad/Gerber design defines the copper geometry but does not fully document any post-fabrication electrode plating or surface treatment.

Order the surface finish as "immersion silver (Ag)" or the manufacturer equivalent.

## Connector

The current design uses a 1×16, 2.54 mm pitch horizontal header footprint.

Pins 1–15 connect to the 15 electrode pads. Pin 16 does not have a corresponding round electrode in the current board layout.

Be very careful while soldering the pins, the transparent PCB material is generally extremely sensitive to high heat.


## KiCad version

The supplied PCB file was generated by KiCad 9.0.

The custom symbol/footprint libraries are included locally.
