# Experimental Protocols

This directory contains representative experimental protocols and
workflows used with the HEartLab multimodal mapping platform.

These documents should be interpreted as **examples of implementations
used in HEartLab experiments rather than universal or mandatory
experimental procedures**.

The platform is modular, and experimental procedures may vary according
to the scientific objective, preparation type, species or donor source,
heart size, mapping modalities, perfusion configuration, available
equipment, and approved experimental protocol.

Parameters that may vary between experiments include, where appropriate:

- total solution volume;
- perfusion flow;
- perfusion pressure;
- temperature;
- stimulation site and stimulation parameters;
- recording duration and sequence;
- pharmacological interventions;
- drug concentration and total administered amount;
- dye loading;
- motion-suppression strategy;
- electrode and camera positioning;
- number and type of mapping modalities;
- arrhythmia-induction procedures;
- use of 3D reconstruction.

Values reported in individual protocols correspond to representative
HEartLab implementations and should not automatically be assumed to be
appropriate for another preparation.

Users are responsible for verifying all concentrations, calculations,
equipment settings, physiological parameters, electrical connections,
and experimental procedures before use.
