# Large-Heart Transparent-PCB Support

This assembly supports the transparent electrical PCBs used inside the large-heart recording tank.

The support was designed to maintain the PCBs in their intended geometry while limiting bending or mechanical stress on the boards and their fragile electrical connectors.

## Assembly components

### Top internal support

**Quantity:** 1

Supports the upper portion of the transparent-PCB assembly inside the tank.

### Bottom internal support

**Quantity:** 1

Supports the lower portion of the transparent-PCB assembly inside the tank.

### Connections

**Quantity:** 6

Connect the upper and lower internal supports and maintain the overall geometry of the PCB-support assembly.

### Fasteners 

**Quantity:** 12

Attach the connectors to the bottom and top supports (M3x10mm). Can be screwed directly into the printed plastic as it is not load bearing.

### Top external support

Supports the external connector region of the transparent PCB.

Its primary purpose is to reduce bending and mechanical loading near the electrical connector.

## Available files

The repository includes:

- Fusion assembly/source file;
- PDF assembly drawing;
- individual STL files for printable components.

## Not included

The recording tank itself is not included as a custom CAD model in this directory.

The fasteners used to fit the connectors to the top and bottom supports.

The transparent PCB designs are documented separately under the electrical-mapping hardware documentation.

## Assembly considerations

The transparent PCBs are mechanically fragile.

During assembly:

- avoid forcing the boards into the supports;
- avoid excessive bending;
- support the connector region during cable attachment;
- verify that the internal supports do not obstruct the intended optical field of view;
- verify alignment before filling the tank.
