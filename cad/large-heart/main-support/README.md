# Large-Heart Main Support

The main support provides the structural and rotational interface for the large-heart support assembly.

It holds:

- the heart-support rotation shaft;
- the NEMA 14 stepper motor;
- the shaft pulley;
- the motor pulley;
- the timing belt;
- shaft bearings.

The entire main-support assembly is mounted on two lateral 5 mm support shafts. Its vertical position can be adjusted along these shafts and fixed using external clamps.

## Corrosion considerations

The support is used near heated physiological solutions and a humid experimental environment.

Where possible, bearings and metallic components should therefore use corrosion-resistant materials or sealed construction.

Ceramic or appropriately sealed bearings may be advantageous where exposure to moisture is expected.
