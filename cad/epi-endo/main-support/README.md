## Main support assembly

The epi-endo main support provides mechanical stability for the tissue preparation and associated perfusion tubing.

The assembly includes:

- base;
- support legs;
- tubing holder;
- recording tank geometry represented in the assembly.

The tubing holder constrains the cannula tubing and reduces uncontrolled movement. This prevents the weight or motion of the perfusion tubing from displacing or lifting the tissue preparation during an experiment.

### Custom components

| Component | Quantity | Function |
|---|---:|---|
| Base | 1 | Main structural support |
| Legs | 4 | Support the assembly |
| Tubing holder | 1 | Restrains perfusion tubing and reduces load on preparation |

### Available files

The assembly is provided as:

- Fusion archive (`.f3z`);
- STEP model;
- PDF assembly drawing.

Individual printable components are provided as STL files.

### Not included

The cannula itself is not included as part of this CAD assembly.

The fasteners: 12 M3x25mm fasteners + nuts + 24 washers are required to assemble.
