# Small-Heart Serpentine Support

This assembly supports the perfusion heat-exchange serpentine and maintains it in a vertical position during operation.

The support is divided into two principal component types:

1. main serpentine support;
2. serpentine holders.

## Custom components

### Main serpentine support

**Quantity:** 1

Provides the structural interface between the serpentine assembly and the experimental support system.

### Serpentine holder

**Quantity:** 2

Two holders clamp and support the serpentine.

The holders are connected to the main support through hinged interfaces and secured in the closed position using M3 screws and nuts.

## Available files

The assembly is provided as:

- Fusion source;
- STEP assembly.

Individual components are provided as STL files for fabrication.

## Off-the-shelf / non-distributed components

| Component | Quantity | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| M3 screws | As required | Close/secure holders |
| M3 nuts | As required | Used with holder screws |
| Heat-exchange serpentine | 1 | Not included as a custom CAD model |

## Assembly considerations

The support should hold the serpentine firmly without applying excessive force to the tubing.

Before use:

- verify that both holders close correctly;
- verify hinge movement;
- tighten M3 fasteners sufficiently to prevent movement;
- verify that the support does not kink or restrict perfusion tubing.
