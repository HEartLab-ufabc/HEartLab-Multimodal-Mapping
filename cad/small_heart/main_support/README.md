# Small-Heart Main Support

The small-heart main support positions the cannula assembly within the experimental setup and provides controlled adjustment of its position and orientation.

The assembly allows adjustment of:

- distance from the center of the recording tank;
- cannula tilt;
- support position along the primary support rod.

## Custom components

### Main Support

**Quantity:** 1

Holds the rotating cannula assembly.

Available files include:

- Autodesk Inventor source (`.ipt`);
- STEP (`.stp`);
- STL.

### Main Support Holder

**Quantity:** 1

Connects the main support to a 13 mm support rod.

The holder allows the position and angle of the main support to be adjusted.

Threaded inserts are used to secure the adjustment interfaces:

| Position | Insert |
|---|---|
| Top | M4 |
| Bottom | M4 |
| Center | M5 |

A longer M4 screw and nut are used to clamp the holder around the 13 mm support rod.

### Support Addon

A spacer/interface component used with the main-support holder and additional accessories, including the serpentine-support assembly.

## Adjustment

The support allows the cannula to be repositioned without rebuilding the entire experimental setup.

Before use, verify that:

- all adjustment screws are secure;
- the main support cannot rotate unintentionally;
- the cannula rotates freely;
- the support does not interfere with cameras, electrodes, or tank walls.
